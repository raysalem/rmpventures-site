03 / about me

Hi, I'm Milad Salem.

Owner & Principal, RMP Ventures

RMP Ventures has been a family business since 2011, and I have run it for the past ten years on a simple idea: well-run homes make good investments and happy residents. In that decade the business has grown dramatically, built on seeing opportunities early, knowing the market, and making the call when the right property comes along.

With more than ten years in real estate, I have managed a growing portfolio of residential rentals across San Diego County, from single-family homes to multi-unit buildings. That means the whole job: marketing and leasing, turnovers and renovations, permits and utility upgrades, vendor relationships, and the late-night maintenance call.

On the acquisition side I help find, evaluate and close new properties, and plan the improvements that make them better places to live. Today RMP Ventures is organized as an LLC and also works alongside fellow owners and investors through consulting and private debt and equity partnerships.

When I'm not walking a property, you'll find me with my family, usually planning the next outing or gathering.
